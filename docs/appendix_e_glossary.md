# GAD v1.2: Governed Adaptive Delivery
**Subtitle:** A Human-Centered Hybrid Method for AI-Assisted, Governed, Adaptive Delivery  
**Method Version:** 1.2  
**Documentation Release:** 1.1  
**Author:** David Betzold  
**Publication Baseline:** 6 September 2026  
**Status:** Corrected modular book documentation release

> GAD combines deliberate milestone planning, adaptive Agile execution, AI-assisted delivery, and explicit human authority. The method is designed to gain the speed and analytical leverage of AI without allowing convenience, confidence, or automation to silently become authority.


# Appendix E — Glossary

**A0–A4** — GAD authorization levels from observation to high-impact/authority-changing action.

**Agent** — An AI-enabled capability or autonomous/semi-autonomous software role operating inside defined boundaries.

**Approval-ready package** — A concise decision package containing proposal, evidence, risk, uncertainty, and required decision.

**Authorization** — Permission for a named actor or action class to act within a defined scope and context.

**Autonomy** — Ability to execute independently inside an already authorized operating boundary.

**Baseline** — An approved reference for scope, architecture, budget, milestone, policy, or another governed commitment.

**Blocked** — State indicating progress cannot continue until an identified dependency or condition is resolved.

**Decision Record** — Evidence of a consequential human or authorized governance decision.

**Evidence** — Verifiable information supporting a claim, status, decision, or outcome.

**Evidence Package** — Structured collection summarizing technical result, sufficiency, deviations, limitations, readiness, and approval.

**Fail Closed** — Refuse to cross a control boundary when mandatory authorization, evidence, validation, or prerequisites are missing.

**Gate** — Explicit checkpoint determining whether a stage, release, pilot, or authority transition may proceed.

**GAD** — Governed Adaptive Delivery.

**Governance Debt** — Accumulated weakness or manual burden in controls, policies, permissions, evidence, or decision structures.

**Human Decision** — Workflow state or governance point where human authority is required.

**Human Project Hub** — The named human decision authority for consequential decisions not explicitly delegated.

**Historical Truth** — Preservation of actual past results even when later remediation changes current readiness.

**Remediation Required** — Explicit outcome indicating a mandatory control failed or remains unsatisfied.

**Risk** — Potential consequence and uncertainty associated with an action; risk alone does not determine authorization.

**Standing Delegation** — Pre-approved authority for a bounded repeatable class of actions, typically A2.

**Validation** — Independent or appropriately separated evaluation that defined criteria are satisfied.

**WIP Limit** — Maximum allowed work in a flow state to protect throughput and focus.

## AI-Agent Terminology

**AI-to-AI handoff** — Transfer of work from one AI capability to another. It does not transfer human authorization.

**Confidence band** — A locally calibrated interval used to interpret model confidence for review/monitoring; it does not grant authority.

**Confidence score** — Model/system estimate of confidence or certainty. It is evidence about prediction quality, not permission.

**Hallucination guardrail** — Control intended to detect, constrain, or route unsupported AI output.

**Human-in-the-loop** — A human must actively approve or complete a defined step before the system proceeds.

**Human-on-the-loop** — A human supervises a bounded automated process and can intervene under defined controls.

**Model version pinning** — Use or recording of an identifiable model version so governed execution can be traced and regression assessed.

**Orchestrator** — Capability that coordinates bounded work, dependencies, and handoffs without gaining approval authority by coordination.

**Planner** — Capability that decomposes, forecasts, and recommends.

**RAG grounding** — Retrieval-augmented generation using identified source material; grounding improves traceability but does not by itself validate truth or grant authority.

**Unauthorized action** — Action executed outside valid authorization, scope, conditions, or validity period.

**Worker** — Capability that performs bounded execution.

**Validator** — Capability that evaluates defined criteria with sufficient separation from execution for the consequence involved.
